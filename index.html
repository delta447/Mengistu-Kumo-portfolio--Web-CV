About Me

I am a dedicated academic and technical leader with extensive experience spanning over 17 years in construction technology, project planning & management, and TVET (Technical and Vocational Education and Training) leadership. My career has been marked by progressive roles in public sector administration, from technical positions to senior management and academic leadership.

Currently serving as Dean of Yirgalem Polytechnic College, I bring a comprehensive understanding of institutional management, educational administration, and technical expertise. My background in construction technology and project management, combined with hands-on experience in municipal administration and urban development, enables me to lead with both strategic vision and practical knowledge.

Throughout my career, I have been committed to advancing technical education, improving institutional capacity, and contributing to Ethiopia's development through effective leadership and management practices.
